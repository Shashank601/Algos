Pattern to follow when practicing

For each game:
Define allowed moves.
Compute small Grundy values (n = 0 up to ~20).
Detect pattern or periodicity.
Derive a formula (if possible).
Apply for large n.
This workflow works on all problems in the resources above.


After a few examples, you realize Grundy numbers form patterns / mod formulas.
This turns a “recursive, huge computation” problem (like 2.4 billion pile) into O(1) formula problems.
